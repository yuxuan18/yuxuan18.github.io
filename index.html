yxx404@g.illinois.edu

I am a first-year Ph.D. student at UIUC advised by Prof. Daniel Kang. My research focuses on the intersection of machine learning and data systems, enabling efficient approximate query processing over unstructured data. During my undergraduate study at the University of Michigan, I worked with Mosharaf Chowdhury and Fan Lai on federated learning. I also worked with Xinyu Wang on the optimization and testing of SQL queries.
