Yuxuan Zhu

yxx404@g.illinois.edu

I am a second-year Ph.D. student at UIUC advised by Prof. Daniel Kang. I am interested in data + AI/ML. My research focuses on developing statistically grounded approach to enable efficient data analytics, rigorous AI evaluations, and AI for safety. I also work closely with Fan Lai.

Before that, I was an undergraduate at the University of Michigan. I worked with Prof. Mosharaf Chowdhury on federated learning and Prof. Xinyu Wang on the optimization and testing of SQL queries.
